Final Year Project · CSIT321 · SIM–UOW

News from people, checked before it's published.

Mainstream newsrooms are trustworthy but closed. Open platforms let anyone publish but nobody checks. The Daily Loop is the first that is both participatory and accountable — anyone can apply to write, every writer is verified, and every article is reviewed before it goes live.

Request a demo See the plans

The problem we set out to solve

Every major news site publishes as a flat stream. When a story develops over weeks — a court case, a redevelopment, a policy rollout — following it means searching repeatedly and reassembling the chronology in your head. We reviewed seven news platforms and found none that group their coverage by event.

What makes it different

Story Timeline

Follow an event, not articles

Every article and update about one developing event sits on a single chronological timeline you can search by event name.

Verified authors

Open to the public, still checked

Writers apply, prove who they are, and are approved by an editor. Every article they submit is still reviewed before publication.

Transparency

We show our working

Each story states whether it was organised automatically or checked by an editor. Perspective is declared, not hidden.

Plans

Free

Everything that makes us different

Read every article, search by event, follow any story timeline, comment and react. Supported by advertising. The timeline is never behind a paywall.

Pro

For people following stories closely

No advertising, plus a catch-up summary of what changed while you were away, the people and organisations behind each story, audio playback and alerts on the stories you follow.
