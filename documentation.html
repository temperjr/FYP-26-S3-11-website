Project documentation

A running record of what we have produced and where the project stands. This page exists so the team, our supervisor and our assessor can see progress in one place.

Documents are not published on this page. Assessment work is held in the group's access-controlled shared drive and submitted through Moodle. Team members and teaching staff: request access through the project lead.
